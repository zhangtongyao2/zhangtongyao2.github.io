Tongyao Zhang

Hi! I'm Tongyao Zhang. I'm currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences. I work with Dr. Ben Motz at the Change Lab.

I received my Ph.D. in Developmental Psychology and Cognitive Science working with Dr. Emily Fyfe. My dissertation focused on children's early patterning skill and its relationship with cognitive development, especially numerical knowledge. Please check out this critical review for my research rationales and prospects.

Research interests

My research examines learning processes and explores the nature of knowledge and intelligence. I am interested in questions such as: How do children understand patterns, relations, and numbers? How does the design of the learning materials shape learners' conceptual understanding? What does the interaction between human learners and AI learning tools tell us about human's reasoning, conceptualization, and memory, and those of AI's?

  • Cognitive development and learning
  • Reasoning and higher-order cognition
  • AI in education
